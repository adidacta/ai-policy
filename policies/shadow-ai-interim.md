Stopgap guidance issued before a full acceptable-use policy existed. Retained
for the audit trail only.

## Policy

1. Staff were asked to avoid entering any company information into AI tools that
   had not been cleared by IT.
2. Requests for new tools were routed to the IT helpdesk.
