## Purpose

Define what staff may and may not do with AI tools, so that useful work is not
slowed down by ambiguity and risky work does not happen by accident.

## Scope

Every employee and contractor, using any AI tool, for any work purpose —
including tools they pay for personally.

## Policy

1. AI-assisted output is the author's output. Whoever ships it is accountable
   for its accuracy, licensing, and tone, exactly as if they had written it.
2. Confidential or personal data may only be entered into tools listed in the
   approved register. See [[data-handling]] for what counts as confidential.
3. AI use must be disclosed where the reader's judgement depends on knowing:
   customer-facing writing, code review, research findings, hiring materials.
4. Generated code is reviewed by a human before merge. Volume is not a reason to
   lower review standards.
5. Tools not on the register may be used for public, non-sensitive information
   only. Requesting an addition to the register is encouraged, not discouraged.

## Exceptions

Raise a request with the policy owner. Time-boxed exceptions for evaluation and
pilots are routine and usually granted.

## Review

Reviewed twice yearly — the tool landscape moves faster than the annual cycle.
